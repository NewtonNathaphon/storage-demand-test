## Pricing mechanism and right-sizing examples

The LEO annual offer exchanges a discount for a 12-month commitment and 100% upfront payment. Proposed StorageBuddy philosophy: full monthly rate; modest benefit for three months; larger benefit for six months; strongest benefit for annual prepayment. Promotions should eventually depend on size-specific demand: avoid discounting nearly full 3–4 sqm rooms while testing promotions for slow-moving 8 sqm rooms.

The supplied analysis says prepayment eliminates vacancy/collection risk for the term. More precisely, collected prepayment reduces collection exposure and improves cash timing; contractual refunds, service obligations, cancellations and later vacancy remain relevant. Upfront cash is not all earned monthly revenue immediately. Do not implement discount rules or contract terms from this note.

Underwriting should distinguish list price from realized occupied-unit rent after discounts, and apply occupancy separately to rentable inventory to avoid double-counting vacancy. Record promotions, term and prepayment distinctly.

Example supplied: a customer comparing LEO 4 sqm at THB 4,500 could instead use a suitable StorageBuddy 3 sqm room at THB 3,290, saving THB 1,210/month. The arithmetic is correct, but price and physical fit are illustrative and must be confirmed. This is the commercial meaning of right sizing, not a published savings guarantee.
