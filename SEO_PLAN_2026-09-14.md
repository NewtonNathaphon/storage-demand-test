# StorageBuddy organic-search plan

Status: proposed implementation plan; not public website copy.
Scope: StorageBuddy’s approximately 1,500 sqm professional self-storage concept centered on Rama 3.

## Goal

Generate qualified organic enquiries for Node #1—not simply traffic. Optimize for customers who need rentable storage in the Rama 3 catchment, then measure enquiry quality, required area, duration, move-in timing and eventual conversion.

## Current audit

### What already works

- HTTPS site is live and crawlable.
- `robots.txt` allows crawling and points to the sitemap.
- `sitemap.xml` is live.
- Homepage has a canonical URL, title, meta description, one H1 and Organization JSON-LD.[1]
- Thai-first responsive content, size guidance and enquiry conversion flows already exist.

### Priority gaps

1. Public index checks were inconsistent: one `site:` lookup returned no pages while a later lookup returned the homepage. Search Console must be the source of truth for discovery, indexing and exclusions.
2. No Search Console verification tag or public DNS verification record was detected.
3. The homepage contains Rama 3 wording but does not naturally use the exact high-intent Thai phrases `ห้องเก็บของให้เช่า`, `เช่าห้องเก็บของ` or `เช่าที่เก็บของ`.
4. `/rooms` currently duplicates the homepage while declaring itself canonical. It should become a genuinely useful size/price page or redirect to the homepage; it should not remain a duplicate sitemap entry.
5. English is switched with JavaScript on the same URL. Create separate crawlable Thai and English URLs, then connect equivalents with `hreflang`; Google recommends distinct URLs for localized versions.[5]
6. The page is primarily a conversion landing page with limited indexable informational depth and almost no crawlable internal page network.
7. Several published PNG assets are approximately 1.6–1.9 MB each. Convert to responsive WebP/AVIF assets to reduce mobile transfer and protect page experience.
8. `www.storagebuddyth.com` serves a duplicate host instead of redirecting permanently to the canonical apex host.
9. Organization schema exists, but LocalBusiness/storage-facility markup should wait until the customer-facing address and operating facts are accurate and public. Google requires accurate physical-location information for LocalBusiness data.[6]

## Search strategy

Do not try to rank nationally for every storage phrase first. Win Rama 3 and nearby intent, then expand based on real enquiry geography.

### Tier 1: transactional Rama 3 terms

- ห้องเก็บของให้เช่า พระราม 3
- เช่าห้องเก็บของ พระราม 3
- เช่าที่เก็บของ พระราม 3
- self storage พระราม 3
- self storage Rama 3
- ห้องเก็บของรายเดือน พระราม 3
- ที่เก็บของใกล้ฉัน

These should lead the homepage/location page, title, H1, introductory copy, internal anchors and conversion copy naturally—not as a repeated keyword list.

### Tier 2: nearby catchment terms

Use only where the page gives genuinely useful local information:

- ห้องเก็บของให้เช่า สาธุประดิษฐ์
- ห้องเก็บของให้เช่า ยานนาวา
- ห้องเก็บของให้เช่า บางคอแหลม
- ห้องเก็บของให้เช่า สาทร
- ที่เก็บของ เจริญกรุง
- self storage near Sathorn

Do not publish near-identical doorway pages for every district. Start with one substantial Rama 3 location page that explains roads, landmarks, loading access and realistic service radius.

### Tier 3: use-case intent

- ที่เก็บของย้ายบ้าน / เก็บของระหว่างย้ายบ้าน
- ที่เก็บเฟอร์นิเจอร์ระหว่างรีโนเวท
- ที่เก็บสต๊อกสินค้าออนไลน์
- โกดังขนาดเล็กให้เช่า
- ที่เก็บเอกสารบริษัท
- ห้องเก็บของสำหรับคอนโด
- business inventory storage Bangkok
- furniture storage Bangkok

`โกดังให้เช่า` has broader search interest but can imply a full warehouse. Use it on a business-storage page with clear room sizes and limitations rather than making it the brand’s main promise.[9]

## Recommended page architecture

### Phase-one commercial pages

1. `/` — Thai Rama 3 commercial homepage
   - Proposed title: `ห้องเก็บของให้เช่า พระราม 3 | StorageBuddy Self Storage`
   - Proposed H1 if the facility remains pre-opening: `เตรียมเปิดห้องเก็บของให้เช่า พระราม 3 สำหรับบ้านและธุรกิจ`
   - If the facility is confirmed and bookable, remove “เตรียมเปิด” and publish accurate opening/address facts.

2. `/en/` — English homepage
   - Title: `Self Storage Rama 3, Bangkok | StorageBuddy`
   - Unique English copy, canonical and Thai/English `hreflang` pair.

3. `/sizes/` and `/en/sizes/`
   - Unique 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 8 sqm explanations.
   - What fits, illustrative dimensions, who each size serves and CTA.
   - Add approved prices only when commercially confirmed.
   - Replace the current duplicate `/rooms` or redirect it permanently.

4. `/location/rama-3/` and `/en/location/rama-3/`
   - Exact public location when confirmed.
   - Access roads, nearby landmarks, parking/loading, transit, map, hours and contact.
   - Original facility photographs and directions.

5. `/personal-storage/`
   - Condo clutter, moving, renovation, furniture and seasonal belongings.

6. `/business-storage/`
   - Stock, documents and equipment; naturally address `โกดังขนาดเล็กให้เช่า` intent without pretending to offer a full logistics warehouse.

### Supporting content

Publish only content that answers real buying questions:

- `ค่าเช่าห้องเก็บของในกรุงเทพคิดอย่างไร`
- `เลือกห้องเก็บของ 1–8 ตร.ม. ให้พอดีกับของ`
- `ย้ายบ้านหรือรีโนเวทคอนโด ควรเช่าที่เก็บของแบบไหน`
- `ร้านค้าออนไลน์ควรเลือกห้องเก็บสต๊อกสินค้ากี่ตารางเมตร`
- `เช็กอะไรบ้างก่อนเช่าห้องเก็บของ: ความปลอดภัย การเข้าถึง และประกัน`

Each article should link to the relevant size, location and enquiry page and include original StorageBuddy examples rather than generic AI filler.

## Competitor benchmark

LEO’s Rama 3 page supplies exact address, unit range, 24/7 access, security, insurance, prices and booking links.[7] Nearby NKP supplies local-distance details, service periods and a starting monthly price.[8]

StorageBuddy does not need to copy their visual identity, but it must eventually answer the same commercial questions. Its differentiators should remain Buddy service, right-sizing help, a clear professional experience and evidence from the actual facility.

## 90-day execution

### Days 1–7: discovery and indexability

- Authorize and verify a Google Search Console Domain property.
- Submit `https://storagebuddyth.com/sitemap.xml` and inspect the homepage live URL. Search Console provides crawl/index status and lets owners request indexing.[2]
- Fix `/rooms` duplication and `www` canonical-host behavior.
- Add automated checks for unique title, description, H1, canonical and structured data on every public page.
- Confirm whether the facility is still pre-opening before changing commercial claims.

Google accepts sitemap submission through Search Console, but submission is a discovery hint rather than a guarantee of indexing.[3]

### Days 8–21: high-intent foundations

- Improve homepage Thai title/H1/body around Rama 3 transactional language.
- Launch separate `/en/` URL and reciprocal `hreflang`.
- Build unique size and Rama 3 location pages.
- Update sitemap with accurate `lastmod` values and only canonical public URLs.
- Convert large PNGs to responsive WebP/AVIF and retest mobile rendering.

### Days 22–45: use-case coverage

- Launch personal-storage and business-storage pages.
- Publish the size-selection guide and moving/renovation guide.
- Add crawlable internal navigation and contextual links.
- Track `utm_source=google_organic` or equivalent first-party attribution through saved enquiries.

### Days 46–90: local authority

- Once the facility is eligible and publicly visitable, create/verify one accurate Google Business Profile with consistent name, address, phone, website, hours, category, photos and attributes. Google says local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and complete accurate profiles are more likely to appear.[4]
- Add real facility/construction photos as appropriate.
- Seek legitimate local links from moving companies, renovation partners, condo/community partners, business associations and relevant storage organizations.
- Ask actual customers for honest Google reviews after service begins; never manufacture reviews.
- Refresh pages from Search Console query data rather than guessing new topics.

## Measurement

Review weekly during launch and monthly after stabilization:

- Indexed canonical pages
- Search impressions and clicks
- Non-brand queries containing storage + location/use case
- Average ranking by page/query, used directionally
- Organic enquiry count
- Qualified organic enquiries
- Requested sqm, duration and move-in timing
- Organic enquiry-to-reservation and reservation-to-move-in rate
- Local Business Profile calls, directions and website visits once eligible

Success is qualified occupancy demand for Node #1, not article count or raw traffic.

## Decisions required before public copy changes

1. Is the 1,500 sqm building contract/location now confirmed?
2. Is the public customer address ready to publish?
3. Expected opening month/date?
4. Which facts are confirmed: 24/7 access, CCTV, keycard, air-conditioning, fire protection, insurance, parking/loading and moving help?
5. Are prices approved for 1/2/3/4/5/8 sqm?

Until confirmed, SEO copy must accurately say planned/pre-opening and collect interest; it must not imply inventory or immediate move-in.

## Acceptance criteria

- Search Console ownership verified and sitemap processed without blocking errors.
- Every indexable URL has unique Thai or English content, title, H1, description, canonical and correct language signals.
- No duplicate `/rooms` homepage.
- Apex host is canonical; alternate host redirects consistently.
- Structured contact data matches visible contact data.
- High-intent Rama 3 terms appear naturally on the appropriate commercial pages.
- Mobile page transfer and key image sizes are materially reduced without visible quality loss.
- Organic enquiries preserve source attribution and can be reviewed in the existing back office.
